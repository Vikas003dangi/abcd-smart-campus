About ABCD Coaching & Library

ABCD Coaching & Library is a focused learning space where English grammar coaching and serious self-study come together. Since 2013, we’ve helped students build strong English, confidence and exam discipline – and since 2017 we’ve provided a peaceful, full-day library from 8:00 AM to 8:30 PM.

Founder & Head Faculty: Sandeep Raghuwanshi Basoda, Madhya Pradesh Coaching · Library · Guidance Established 2013 · Library since 2017

Our Story

ABCD Coaching & Library began with a simple belief: "Any Body Can Do" when they get clear teaching and a calm place to study.

Under the guidance of Mr. Sandeep Raghuwanshi, we have helped students improve their English, build confidence and prepare for competitive and school exams with proper study discipline.

🎓 Coaching

  • Strong foundation in English grammar
  • Concept-based teaching – not just cramming
  • Regular practice, testing and feedback
  • Small batches for personal attention

📚 Library

  • Peaceful, dedicated reading hall
  • Neat, clean and well-ventilated environment
  • Filtered water & basic facilities
  • Flexible study hours (8:00 AM – 8:30 PM)

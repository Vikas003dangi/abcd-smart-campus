Terms of Service

Please review these Terms of Service governing enrollment, facility usage, code of conduct, and online platform access at ABCD Coaching & Library, Ganj Basoda.

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing our campus facilities or utilizing our online portal at https://abcdcampus.in (including digital seat reservations, educational video modules, and communication tools), you agree to comply with and be bound by these Terms of Service.

2. Library Study Hall Discipline

To ensure a focused, respectful, and silent environment for competitive exam preparation:

  • Strict Silence: Absolute silence must be maintained in all study hall areas. Phone calls, discussions, and audible alerts are strictly prohibited inside study zones.
  • Desk Allocation: Desks are allocated on a dedicated basis per your registered shift (Morning, Evening, or Full-Day). Unauthorized swapping or occupancy of unassigned desks is not permitted without administrative approval.
  • Facility Amenities: Students must maintain cleanliness, handle electrical switches and fixtures responsibly, and preserve the quiet environment.

3. Academic Coaching & Intellectual Property

  • Course materials, lecture recordings, syllabus sheets, and mock test papers provided by ABCD Coaching & Library are the intellectual property of the institute and are licensed solely for the personal educational use of enrolled students.
  • Redistribution, public uploading, or commercial resale of proprietary study materials is prohibited.

4. Guidy & Digital Platform Code of Conduct

  • Our digital communication tools (including Guidy chat, batch channels, and question forums) are provided strictly for constructive academic discussions, doubts resolution, and peer mentorship.
  • Harassment, abusive language, inappropriate media sharing, or spamming will result in immediate suspension of digital access.

# Walkthrough: Email Architecture & Contact Prompt Fixes

We have implemented the separation of **Account/Credentials Email** and **Contact/Notification Email**, ensuring that user account emails are never overwritten when updating profile contact details, while resolving the issue where the missing contact modal was unnecessarily prompted to approved students on their dashboard.

---

## Key Changes Made

### 1. Form Level (`users/forms.py`)
- **[StudentProfileForm](file:///b:/ABCD/abcd_web/users/forms.py)**:
  - Pre-populates `initial['email']` with existing `profile.email` or `user.email`.
  - In `save()`, saves the provided contact email to `StudentProfile.email` (or defaults to `user.email` if none was set previously).
- **[EditStudentProfileForm](file:///b:/ABCD/abcd_web/users/forms.py)**:
  - Added `'email'` to `Meta.fields`.
  - Updated `save()` so that changing the contact email writes to `StudentProfile.email` without modifying `student.user.email`.
- **[StudentAchievementForm](file:///b:/ABCD/abcd_web/users/forms.py)**:
  - Pre-populates `initial['email']` with existing `achievement.email`, `profile.email`, or `user.email`.
- **[EditAlumniProfileForm](file:///b:/ABCD/abcd_web/users/forms.py)**:
  - Updated `save()` so that changing the contact email writes to `StudentAchievement.email` without modifying `achievement.user.email`.

### 2. View Level (`users/views.py`)
- **[admission_form_view](file:///b:/ABCD/abcd_web/users/views.py)**:
  - Pre-fills `initial['email']` with `request.user.email` for new admission submissions.
  - Automatically defaults `student_profile.email` to `request.user.email` if the user leaves the email field blank on submission.
- **[achievement_form_view](file:///b:/ABCD/abcd_web/users/views.py)**:
  - Defaults `achievement.email` to `profile.email` or `request.user.email` if left blank.
- **[update_contact_info_api](file:///b:/ABCD/abcd_web/users/views.py)**:
  - Saves the contact email to `profile.email` (and syncs to `achievement.email`).
  - Only sets `user.email` if `user.email` was previously empty (e.g., manual student without login email).
- **[send_fee_receipt](file:///b:/ABCD/abcd_web/users/views.py)** & **Broadcasts**:
  - Uses `get_user_notification_email(stud)` so all students receive fee receipts and broadcasts at their active contact email.

### 3. Notification Dispatch Routing (`users/notifications.py` & `users/utils.py`)
- **[users/notifications.py](file:///b:/ABCD/abcd_web/users/notifications.py)**:
  - Updated `send_seat_switch_approval_email`, `send_seat_rejection_email`, and `send_student_progress_email` to route via `get_user_notification_email(student)`.
- **[users/utils.py](file:///b:/ABCD/abcd_web/users/utils.py)**:
  - Updated broadcast loop, `_fire_reminder`, and `process_learning_reminders` to resolve recipient addresses using `get_user_notification_email()`.

### 4. Template Level (`users/templates/users/student_dashboard.html`)
- **[student_dashboard.html](file:///b:/ABCD/abcd_web/users/templates/users/student_dashboard.html)**:
  - Updated the lock popup conditional statement to `{% if profile.status == 'admitted' and not profile.email and not user.email or profile.status == 'admitted' and not profile.whatsapp_number %}`.
  - Students who registered with Google or standard registration are no longer asked to provide their email upon admission approval. The popup only appears if both contact and account emails are missing, or if WhatsApp number is missing (e.g., when created manually by a teacher).

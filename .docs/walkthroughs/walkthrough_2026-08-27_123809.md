## 🌐 Part 2: How to Deploy Live & Connect `abcd2013.online`

### Option A: Deploy on Render.com (Recommended - Easiest with Free Tier)

1. Go to [Render.com](https://render.com) and sign up with your **GitHub** account.
2. Click **"New +"** in the top right and select **"Blueprint"**.
3. Select your `abcd-web-platform` repository.
4. Render will automatically detect `render.yaml` and show:
   - Web Service: `abcd-web-platform` (running Daphne ASGI).
   - Database: `abcd-postgres-db` (PostgreSQL).
5. Click **"Apply"** to start the automatic build!
6. In the Web Service settings, go to the **Environment** tab and add any missing secrets from `.env.example` (such as `EMAIL_HOST_USER`, `EMAIL_HOST_PASSWORD`, `WHATSAPP_API_TOKEN`, etc.).

---

### Step 3: Connect Your Custom Domain (`abcd2013.online`)

1. In your Render Dashboard (or Railway/VPS), navigate to your web service and click **"Settings" > "Custom Domains"**.
2. Click **"Add Custom Domain"** and enter:
   - `abcd2013.online`
   - `www.abcd2013.online`
3. Render will display DNS instructions:
   - **CNAME Record:** Host `www` -> Points to `abcd-web-platform.onrender.com`
   - **A Record:** Host `@` (root) -> Points to Render's IP address (e.g., `216.24.57.1`).
4. Open the website where you purchased `abcd2013.online` (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Hostinger, etc.):
   - Go to **DNS Management** / **DNS Zone Editor**.
   - Add the **CNAME** and **A** records provided by Render.
5. Within 5–15 minutes, Render will automatically issue a **free SSL certificate (HTTPS)**. Your site will be securely accessible at `https://abcd2013.online`!

## ⚙️ Part 3: How Background Automation Works Online

Your background scheduler ([run_local_scheduler.py](file:///b:/ABCD/abcd_web/users/management/commands/run_local_scheduler.py)) handles:
- Real-time scheduled broadcast dispatches.
- To-Do auto-trash recycling.
- Daily fee expiry reminders & WhatsApp warnings.
- 3-day library seat hold grace periods & waitlist auto-promotions.
- Student birthday wishes.
- Automated media cleanup (10-day expired chat media, resolved complaint images).

### How to Run it in Production:
- **Method 1 (Dedicated Worker Service - Included in `Procfile`):**
  On hosts like Render or Railway, you can create a background worker that executes `python manage.py run_local_scheduler`. It runs continuously 24/7.
- **Method 2 (Render Cron Job or cron-job.org):**
  Trigger `python manage.py run_local_scheduler --once` via a cron job once every hour or day to run daily maintenance tasks.
